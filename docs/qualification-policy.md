# Qualification policy guide

A production policy should define at least:

- minimum global and per-class recall for safety-relevant classes;
- maximum calibration error;
- maximum acceptable degradation for each approved perturbation;
- OOD confidence/rejection expectations;
- maximum group-performance gap and justified grouping attributes;
- target-specific P50, P95 and worst-case latency;
- memory and power budgets;
- drift thresholds and monitoring actions;
- required human roles, independence and electronic approval;
- model, dataset, runtime, compiler and hardware identities;
- rollback and incident-response criteria.

The included thresholds are demonstration fixtures, not automotive safety requirements.
